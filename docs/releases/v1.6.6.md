## 007 Remastered 1.6.6: hit flash can be turned off

- **A new Hit flash setting** in the launcher's Settings > Controls > Gameplay turns off the white screen flash
  when Bond is hit, for **NightFire** and **Agent Under Fire**. It is on by default; turn it off if it troubles your
  eyes.

Downloads: Windows launcher (.exe, both games), Linux launcher (.AppImage), macOS launcher (.dmg) and the Android app (.apk).

No game files are included: the launcher builds the games from your own Xbox disc.
