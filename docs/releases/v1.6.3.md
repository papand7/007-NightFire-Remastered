## 007 Remastered 1.6.3: one renderer everywhere, the launcher finds your disc again

- **The launcher finds your disc again.** 1.6.2 showed "No game yet" even after you picked a correct disc image, for both games. Fixed.
- **Vulkan is now the only renderer**, on Windows, Linux, macOS and Android. The Direct3D 12 and 11 renderers are gone. Both games already drew with Vulkan, so nothing changes in play. A PC without a Vulkan 1.3 driver now gets a message saying so.
- What only Direct3D had now works in Vulkan on every platform: the **sharp scaling filter**, **enhanced world textures**, and frames shown as they are paced on screens whose refresh rate is not a multiple of 60. The MSAA option is removed; SMAA, TAA and render scale remain.
- **Linux, macOS and Android** gain what only Windows had: **smooth turning** on 100 Hz and faster screens (the refresh rate is now detected), the **HDR** screen's brightness, the **screenshot key**, the hidden pointer in play, steady LAN play pacing, the LAN lobby's status in the window title (Escape plays alone), and the boot splash. **DLSS** now works on **Linux** too (NVIDIA RTX).
- The **Android app** is now called **007 Remastered** (`007Remastered-Android.apk`). It shows both games from the start, each with how to get its package onto the phone. Installing it over the old app keeps your saves.

Downloads: Windows launcher (.exe, both games), Linux launcher (.AppImage), macOS launcher (.dmg) and the Android app (.apk).

Not tested in play yet on macOS, and on a real phone. No game files are included: the launcher builds the games from your own Xbox disc.
